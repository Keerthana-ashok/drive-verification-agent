# Example Drives warranty policy (fictional)

This is a fictional policy written for a portfolio project.

## Section W1: Warranty periods

Enterprise drives carry a 3-year limited warranty from the purchase date. Consumer drives carry a 2-year limited warranty from the purchase date. Refurbished drives of any segment carry a 1-year limited warranty from the purchase date.

## Section W2: Proof of purchase

The purchase date on record is used to calculate warranty coverage. If no purchase date is on record, the support rep must ask the customer for proof of purchase (an invoice or receipt showing the date) before any warranty decision is made.

## Section W3: Eligibility for replacement (RMA)

A drive is eligible for a warranty replacement (RMA) only when all of these are true: the drive passed authenticity verification, the drive is within its warranty period, and the customer reports a failure or defect. Drives outside the warranty period are not eligible for a free replacement.

## Section W4: Approval of replacements

Every RMA replacement must be approved by a warranty reviewer before a replacement order is created. The support rep who opened the case cannot approve it.

## Section W5: Out-of-warranty options

For drives outside the warranty period, the rep may offer paid data recovery services and a 15% discount on a new drive of the same segment.
