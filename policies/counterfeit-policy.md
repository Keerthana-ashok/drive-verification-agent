# Example Drives counterfeit handling policy (fictional)

This is a fictional policy written for a portfolio project.

## Section C1: How authenticity is decided

A drive is genuine only if its X.509 certificate chain validates to the Example Drives root and it passes the challenge-response test. Authenticity is decided by this cryptographic check alone. Visual inspection, labels or customer statements never override a failed check.

## Section C2: When verification fails

If a drive fails authenticity verification, do not process any warranty claim or replacement for it. Flag the case to the fraud team with the serial number, the verification results and the place of purchase, if known.

## Section C3: Talking to the customer

Tell the customer that the drive could not be verified as a genuine Example Drives product and that the case has been passed for review. Never accuse the customer of fraud. Suggest they contact the seller they bought it from.

## Section C4: Confirmation of counterfeit flags

A counterfeit flag must be confirmed by a reviewer before it is sent to the fraud team.
